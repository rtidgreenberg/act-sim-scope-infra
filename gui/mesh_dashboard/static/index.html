How to read these counts

These are rolling 30-second counts, not cumulative totals.

New messages enter as messages older than 30 seconds leave. At a steady message rate, the numbers stay roughly constant. The window slides continuously; it does not reset every 30 seconds.

What does settled mean?

The summary cards show settled received / settled sent. A send is settled once its grace period has elapsed:

  • Reliable: 15-second grace.
  • Best Effort: 2-second grace.

For one flow sending once per second, expect roughly 15 settled Reliable samples or 28 settled Best Effort samples in the window.

Why can the table differ?

The table's Received count also includes newer arrivals still inside the grace period. Lost counts only settled samples still missing. A late arrival can reduce that loss count.

What is Avg recovery?

For Reliable topics, this is the average time from original WAN send to complete WAN receipt for samples received after a confirmed retry. The number in parentheses is the recovered sample count.

First-attempt deliveries and samples still missing are excluded. No confirmed recoveries shows —, not zero. Like the counts, it uses the rolling send window.

Route / topicSentReceivedLostAvg recovery
Scope: passive RTPS observations from the source WAN interface to each destination WAN interface. This is not DDS reader acceptance, LAN forwarding, or application consumption.
Sent: unique complete RTPS data samples first observed leaving the source WAN interface within the rolling window, counted separately per unicast destination node.
Received: those same samples fully observed at that destination WAN interface. Fragments are reassembled; retransmissions and duplicate copies do not increase message counts.
Lost: sent samples not observed complete at the destination after the grace period. A late observation corrects the count while the send remains in the window. Source and destination captures must cover the entire selected window; startup, rotation, restart, or reporting gaps show Unknown until a full clean window is available and are excluded from summary percentages. Undetected capture drops can still mimic network loss.
Channel grouping: writer reliability decoded from DDS discovery, not topic-name guesses. A complete writer discovery record with an omitted reliability parameter uses the standard Reliable default; an explicit Best Effort value overrides it. Missing discovery stays Unknown QoS. Multicast or unmapped destinations are not assigned invented recipients.
Grace period: 15 s for Reliable channels; 2 s for Best Effort and Unknown QoS. These are observation windows, not guarantees of eventual reliable delivery.
Avg recovery: mean original-send-to-complete-receipt time for Reliable samples received after an observed repair request and matching resend at the writer WAN interface. Each sample contributes once, in its original send window, without waiting for the loss grace period. First-attempt deliveries, missing samples, and retries lacking captured request evidence are excluded. Parentheses show the sample count; — means no confirmed recoveries. This includes loss-detection time, not just retransmit latency. Fragmented samples use completion of the full destination sample. Missing capture coverage shows Unknown; separate hosts require synchronized clocks.
